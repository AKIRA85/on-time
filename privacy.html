Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 31, 2025

On Time ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension uses your data.

1. Data Collection and Usage

On Time accesses your Google Calendar data solely for the purpose of identifying upcoming meetings and automatically opening them for you. We define "meeting links" as URLs found within the location or description fields of your calendar events.

  • Google Calendar API: We use the Google Calendar API to fetch your events. This happens directly within your browser.
  • Local Processing: All data processing happens locally on your device. We do not transmit your calendar data to any external servers or third parties.

2. Permissions

We require the following permissions to function:

  • identity: To authenticate with your Google account and access your calendar.
  • alarms: To schedule checks for upcoming meetings.
  • storage: To save your preferences locally (e.g., enable/disable status).

3. Data Storage

We do not store your calendar events permanently. Events are fetched, processed to find links, and then discarded or temporarily held in your browser's memory to schedule the opening action. Preferences are stored in your browser's local sync storage.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer your data to any third parties.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us via our support page on the Chrome Web Store.
